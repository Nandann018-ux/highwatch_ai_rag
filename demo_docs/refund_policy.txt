# PRODUCT REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
Internal Sales Guidelines

## 1. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, provided that less than 20% of the allocated credits have been used. 

## 2. PARTIAL REFUNDS
Partial refunds (50%) are available between 15 and 30 days of purchase. No refunds are granted after 30 days.

## 3. CANCELLATION PROCESS
Subscription cancellations must be submitted via the Billing Portal. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No pro-rata refunds are provided for mid-cycle cancellations.

## 4. EXCEPTIONS
Refunds for technical failures or service downtime exceeding 99.9% in a single month are handled on a case-by-case basis by the Customer Success Team.

## 5. PROCESSING TIME
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
