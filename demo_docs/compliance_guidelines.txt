# COMPLIANCE AND ETHICS GUIDELINES
Regulatory Standards Document

## 1. ANTI-BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION
Employees must not offer, promise, give, or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision. Gifts exceeding $50 in value must be reported to the Compliance Officer.

## 2. DATA PRIVACY (GDPR/CCPA)
All customer data must be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) must be encrypted at rest and in transit.

## 3. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any external business interests that could conflict with their responsibilities at DriveLens. Disclosure forms are due annually by March 31.

## 4. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
Violations or suspected unethical behavior should be reported via the Anonymous Whistleblower Hotline or directly to the Legal Department.

## 5. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
All employees are required to complete Annual Compliance Training by November 30. Failure to complete training may result in temporary suspension of system access.
