# TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Financial Reimbursement Rules

## 1. PRE-APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
All business travel exceeding $500 must be pre-approved by the Department Head. International travel requires additional approval from the CFO.

## 2. AIR TRAVEL
Employees are expected to book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 6 hours in duration.

## 3. LODGING AND MEALS
Daily meal allowance (per diem) is $75 for domestic travel and $100 for international travel. Standard hotel rates should not exceed $250 per night in major cities.

## 4. EXPENSE SUBMISSION
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the completion of travel. Original receipts are required for all expenses exceeding $25.

## 5. REIMBURSEMENT CYCLE
Reimbursements are processed in the next bi-weekly payroll cycle after approval by the Finance Team.
