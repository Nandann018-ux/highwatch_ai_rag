# IT SECURITY STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Technical Security Requirements

## 1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include:
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

## 2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all system logins, including Email, Slack, and Production Cloud Environments. Use of hardware keys (e.g., YubiKey) is preferred.

## 3. WORKSTATION SECURITY
Computers must be locked when leaving your desk. Auto-lock is enforced after 5 minutes of inactivity. Full-disk encryption must be enabled on all company laptops.

## 4. PHISHING AWARENESS
Suspicious emails should be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client. Do not click links or download attachments from unknown senders.

## 5. INCIDENT RESPONSE
In the event of a security breach or lost device, notify the IT Security Team immediately at security@drivelens.io or call the 24/7 Emergency Line.
