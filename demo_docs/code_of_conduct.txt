# CORPORATE CODE OF CONDUCT
Professional Standards and Behavior

## 1. INCLUSIVE WORKPLACE
DriveLens is committed to a diverse and inclusive environment. Discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, religion, or orientation is strictly prohibited.

## 2. PROFESSIONAL INTEGRITY
Employees are expected to act honestly and ethically. This includes accurate time-tracking, honest expense reporting, and respectful communication.

## 3. CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must protect proprietary information, trade secrets, and customer data. Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) remain in effect even after termination of employment.

## 4. SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY
When posting on social media, employees should clarify that their views are personal and not representative of DriveLens. Avoid sharing internal-only documents or photos of office security measures.

## 5. DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, ranging from verbal warnings to immediate termination of employment, depending on the severity of the infraction.
