# LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY
Employee Benefits Overview

## 1. ANNUAL PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of PTO per calendar year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.66 days per month.

## 2. SICK LEAVE / CASUAL LEAVE
Employees are provided with 10 days of paid sick/casual leave per year. These leaves do not carry forward to the next calendar year.

## 3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of fully paid leave.

## 4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
DriveLens observes 11 standard public holidays per year. A list of specific dates is published on the Internal Portal in December for the following year.

## 5. CARRY-FORWARD RULES
A maximum of 5 PTO days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by March 31.
