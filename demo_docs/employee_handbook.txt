# EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK: GENERAL POLICIES
Version 2.4 | Effective January 2024

## 1. MISSION AND VALUES
Our mission is to empower productivity through intelligent data lens. We value integrity, transparency, and high-velocity engineering.

## 2. WORKING HOURS
The standard workweek is 40 hours. Core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Flexible starting and ending times are permitted with manager approval, provided core hours are met.

## 3. PROBATION PERIOD
All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period. Performance reviews are conducted at the 30, 60, and 90-day marks. Successful completion of probation is required for eligibility for the Annual Bonus Program.

## 4. DRESS CODE
We maintain a "Smart Casual" environment. Professional attire is expected during client-facing meetings or external presentations.

## 5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Bi-annual performance reviews occur in June and December. Reviews are linked to compensation adjustments and career progression paths.
